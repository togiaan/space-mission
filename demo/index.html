WHY WE BUILT IT

A great mission is not the biggest rocket. It is the best balance.

Real engineers juggle money, mass, power, signal and time. We wanted kids aged 9 to 14 to feel that challenge with their own hands: try, fail, read the report, and fix one part.

THE SCIENCE INSIDE

Real physics and NASA planet data

Sunlight fades with distance

Solar output falls with the square of the distance. Mars gets about 43% of Earth’s sunlight, Jupiter only a few percent.

Signals fade too

Radio strength falls with the square of the distance to Earth, so outer-planet missions need bigger antennas and more storage.
